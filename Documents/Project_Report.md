# Project Report

## 1. Tong Quan

Monex la ung dung quan ly tai chinh ca nhan tren Android. Ung dung cho phep nguoi dung ghi nhan thu nhap, chi phi, tiet kiem, hoa don, thong bao va xem thong ke tai chinh.

## 2. Van De Giai Quyet

Nguoi dung thuong kho theo doi tien vao, tien ra, cac khoan can tiet kiem va hoa don sap den han. Monex tap trung vao viec ghi nhan nhanh, hien thi ro rang va canh bao dua tren du lieu that.

## 3. Chuc Nang Da Hoan Thanh

- Quan ly tai khoan cuc bo.
- Tach du lieu theo tai khoan.
- Quan ly thu nhap, chi phi va danh muc.
- Quan ly tiet kiem theo muc tieu.
- Quan ly hoa don va loi nhac.
- Thong ke theo thang, nam va danh muc.
- Tro ly chi tieu va thong bao thong minh.
- Xuat PDF/Excel.

## 4. Kien Truc Tong Quan

Ung dung su dung Flutter, chia thanh cac nhom:

- `SRC/monex/lib/data`: model va AppState.
- `SRC/monex/lib/screens`: cac man hinh giao dien.
- `SRC/monex/lib/services`: thong bao, bao cao, home widget, insight.
- `SRC/monex/lib/theme`: mau sac, theme, background.

## 5. Kiem Thu

Da kiem tra:

- Build Android debug.
- Dang ky/dang nhap.
- Them thu nhap/chi phi.
- Them danh muc.
- Tiet kiem nap/rut tien.
- Thong ke theo thang.
- Xuat bao cao.

## 6. Huong Phat Trien

- Them backend.
- Them dong bo cloud.
- Them AI/phan tich chi tieu nang cao.
- Them bieu do va bao cao chi tiet hon.
