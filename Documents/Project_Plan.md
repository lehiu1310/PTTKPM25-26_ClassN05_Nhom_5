# Project Plan

## 1. Muc Tieu

Xay dung ung dung Monex giup nguoi dung quan ly tai chinh ca nhan tren Android, bao gom thu nhap, chi phi, tiet kiem, hoa don, thong bao va thong ke.

## 2. Pham Vi

- Quan ly tai khoan cuc bo.
- Moi tai khoan co du lieu rieng.
- Them va loc giao dich thu/chi.
- Quan ly danh muc giao dich.
- Quan ly tiet kiem va hoa don.
- Thong ke theo thang, nam va danh muc.
- Xuat bao cao PDF/Excel.

## 3. Cong Viec Chinh

| Giai doan | Noi dung | Trang thai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Khoi tao | Tao Flutter project, cau truc man hinh co ban | Hoan thanh |
| Xac thuc | Dang nhap, dang ky, dang xuat, luu tai khoan | Hoan thanh |
| Thu chi | Them thu nhap, chi phi, danh muc, ngay giao dich | Hoan thanh |
| Thong ke | Tong quan, bieu do thang/nam, so sanh ky truoc | Hoan thanh |

## 4. Rủi Ro

- Du lieu dang luu cuc bo, chua co backend nen khong dong bo nhieu thiet bi.
- Can kiem thu them tren nhieu kich thuoc man hinh Android.
- Can bo sung tai lieu sketch va so do thiet ke neu giang vien yeu cau ban ve chi tiet.

## 5. Huong Phat Trien

- Them backend/cloud sync.
- Them xac thuc that bang email.
- Them dashboard phan tich sau hon.
- Them thong bao lap lai cho hoa don dinh ky.
