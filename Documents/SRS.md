# SRS - Software Requirements Specification

## 1. Gioi Thieu

Monex la ung dung quan ly tai chinh ca nhan tren Android. Ung dung tap trung vao viec ghi nhan thu nhap, chi phi, tiet kiem, hoa don va dua ra thong ke theo du lieu cua tung tai khoan.

## 2. Tac Nhan

- Nguoi dung ca nhan.
- Tai khoan khach.
- He thong thong bao cuc bo.

## 3. Yeu Cau Chuc Nang

### 3.1 Quan Ly Tai Khoan

- Nguoi dung co the dang ky tai khoan moi.
- Nguoi dung co the dang nhap bang ten dang nhap/email va mat khau.
- Nguoi dung co the dang xuat.
- Du lieu cua moi tai khoan phai tach rieng.

### 3.2 Quan Ly Giao Dich

- Them thu nhap voi ten, so tien, danh muc va ngay giao dich.
- Them chi phi voi ten, so tien, danh muc va ngay giao dich.
- Cho phep them danh muc thu/chi phat sinh.
- Cho phep nhap giao dich cua thang truoc hoac ngay tuy chon.
- Tim kiem va loc giao dich theo ten, danh muc, loai va thoi gian.

### 3.3 Thong Ke

- Hien thi so du, tong thu nhap va tong chi phi cua thang hien tai.
- Thong ke theo thang va theo nam.
- So sanh thu/chi voi ky truoc.
- Bieu do cot, duong va thong ke danh muc.
- Giao dich cua thang nao chi tinh vao thang do.

### 3.4 Tiet Kiem

- Tao muc tieu tiet kiem.
- Nap tien vao muc tieu nhieu lan.
- Rut tien khi can.
- Hien thi tien do tiet kiem.

### 3.5 Hoa Don Va Nhac Nho

- Tao hoa don/loi nhac.
- Luu ngay den han.
- Hien thong bao cuc bo cho cac muc can xu ly.

### 3.6 Tro Ly Chi Tieu

- Phan tich du lieu hien tai cua tai khoan.
- Canh bao khi chi tieu gan vuot thu nhap hoac ngan sach.
- Khong tao thong bao gia khi chua co du lieu.

### 3.7 Bao Cao

- Xuat bao cao PDF.
- Xuat bao cao Excel.
- Chia se file bao cao qua ung dung khac.

## 4. Yeu Cau Phi Chuc Nang

- Giao dien de dung tren Android Emulator va dien thoai Android.
- Du lieu luu cuc bo bang SharedPreferences.
- Ung dung build duoc bang Android Studio.
- Source code de doc, co chia module theo data, screens, services va theme.

## 5. Gioi Han

- Chua co backend.
- Chua co dong bo cloud.
- Chua co xac thuc email that.
